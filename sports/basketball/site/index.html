PREGAME LINES

Betting

Compare the sportsbook moneyline with Kalshi and Polymarket prices when available. Games with the largest difference in implied win probability appear first.

What do these lines mean?

A moneyline prices who wins outright. A spread is a margin and a total is the combined score. Prediction markets quote prices in cents. The displayed gap scales each source’s two implied prices to add to 100%. Fees, settlement rules, and availability vary.

ON THE COURT

Watch the pick-and-roll

One player screens the ball defender, then cuts toward the rim or moves out for a shot. Watch whether the defense switches assignments or keeps its big player near the basket.

MODERN SPACING

Why teams shoot so many threes

Threats around the arc pull defenders away from the paint. When a drive draws a second defender, look for the pass to an open shooter in the corner.

THE SCOREBOARD

Clock, possessions, and runs

NBA games have four 12-minute quarters. The offense generally has 24 seconds to release a shot that scores or hits the rim. A big lead can shrink quickly if one side makes threes or forces turnovers on consecutive possessions.

THE SEASON

What the NBA Cup changes

The Cup is a tournament within the season. Group play leads to single-elimination rounds. Games also count in the regular-season standings, except for the Cup final.

THE POSTSEASON

Play-In vs. playoffs

Seeds 1–6 in each conference qualify directly. Seeds 7–10 play for the last two spots. Once the playoffs start, each round is a best-of-seven series.

READ THE STATS

What the box score misses

Points, rebounds, and assists are a useful start. Also look for a defender who closes off a drive or a screener who frees a teammate. Neither action has to show up in the box score to change a possession.
