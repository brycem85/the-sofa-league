Betting

Compare sportsbook moneylines and prediction-market prices for the same matchup. Games with the largest difference in normalized implied win probability appear first.

How these prices compare

Sportsbooks quote American odds. Kalshi shows the price to buy a YES contract. Polymarket shows its quoted outcome prices. For the gap, each source’s two implied prices are scaled to add to 100%. Prices can change and may reflect different fees, settlement rules, or regional markets.

Sources include NHL sportsbook listings, Kalshi, and Polymarket.
