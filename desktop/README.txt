THE SOFA LEAGUE — WINDOWS DESKTOP APP
Version 1.6.0 — Broadcast booth

Getting started
1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you want to keep.
   Close any earlier Sofa League window before opening this version.
2. Open The Game Desk.exe from that folder (the original filename preserves existing shortcuts).
3. Use the NHL, NFL, and NBA logos at the top to switch sports.
4. Open Settings in the tab bar, or use File > Settings (Ctrl+,).

Keep all files in this folder together. The app includes its own desktop runtime.

Settings
Choose enabled sports, visible tabs, and page sections.
Turn automatic refresh, team colors, compact layout, and reduced motion on or off.
Changes save immediately. At least one sport must remain enabled.
Use the search box in Settings to find a control or sport quickly.
Both the app's Reduce motion switch and Windows' motion preference are respected.

Updated design
Dark and light Broadcast booth themes, ticket details, and integrated team
colors. Switch appearance from the header or Settings, including Follow device.
Sport icons, game-card contents, scores, and matchup details use coordinated
motion. Unchanged refreshes and initial/cached scores do not celebrate.
Must-Watch includes a Why this game? explanation based on available game data.
The large home banner stays on the Games page so other views open directly
to their content. Shared views stay selected when you switch sports.
This desktop package and the hosted iPhone/web app share the same interface
bundle. Desktop preferences remain on this computer; phone preferences remain
on that phone.

Data and sign-in
Live scores, statistics, odds, remote images, and YouTube video need internet.
The local app opens without a ChatGPT account.
Streaming-provider links open in your default browser, where you sign in with that provider.

Your local settings, team choices, and window preferences are stored under
%APPDATA%\The Game Desk
Pick your teams once in My Team when starting the desktop version.

This app includes Electron. Its license and notices are included in this folder.

Shared Broadcast booth design
The approved Broadcast booth design is shared by NHL, NFL and NBA: a live
score-led Must-Watch feature, a compact schedule and clearer viewing actions.
NHL main-team and followed-team controls are grouped in My Team.

Bold team colors
The existing Team colors option now applies vivid jersey colors to the header,
spotlight, navigation, team banner and whole-hub surfaces in all three sports.
Turn it off to restore the neutral navy/ice theme; your team choice stays saved.
